Finance & Payments

Payment systems that your business and your customers can trust.

PCI DSS compliance, reliable transaction processing, and clean integrations with the gateways and financial services your business depends on.

PCI DSS Compliant Systems
The challenge

Payment infrastructure breaks in ways that are immediately visible to every customer.

A failed checkout, a duplicate charge, a transaction that doesn't reconcile. These aren't just technical problems, they're the ones customers notice first and forgive last. The systems that handle money have to work with a different level of precision than most other software.

Most payment integration problems come from building on top of a gateway without fully understanding what the gateway actually does: how it handles retries, how it communicates failures, how webhooks can arrive out of order or not at all. The gaps in that understanding end up as bugs in production.

We've built payment systems across a range of industries and transaction volumes. The approach is always the same: understand the full data flow before writing any code, handle edge cases explicitly, and build an audit trail that makes every transaction traceable.

PCI DSS — Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard

The global standard for any organization that handles credit card data. Defines requirements for network security, access control, encryption, and regular security testing. Systems we build for payments are designed to meet these requirements from the ground up.

How we approach it

Every transaction needs a complete, traceable record.

The most common payment system failure isn't a crash or an outage. It's a transaction that went through on one side but not the other, and nobody can tell you why. A webhook that arrived twice. A payment marked failed that was actually charged. These silent failures are the hardest to find and the most damaging to customer trust.

Our approach to payment systems starts with the data model: every transaction, every state transition, every webhook event gets stored with a timestamp and a source. The system can always answer what happened to any given payment, and when, and why it reached the state it's in.

Security follows the same principle. Card data is never stored or passed through your systems unless absolutely necessary. We use tokenization and direct integration with PCI-certified vaults, keeping your compliance scope as small as possible.

Security practices we follow

No raw card data in your systems or logs
Tokenization via PCI-certified vault providers
TLS for all payment data in transit
Idempotency keys on all payment requests
Full audit log of every transaction event
Access controls on financial reporting data
