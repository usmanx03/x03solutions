Healthcare 2024 Platform Modernization

MediTrack — Healthcare Platform Modernization

A regional healthcare network was running patient scheduling, billing, and clinical records across three disconnected legacy systems, each over a decade old. Staff were manually reconciling data between platforms, appointments were being lost, and the organization had no unified view of patient history across its 12 clinic locations.

X03 designed and built a unified cloud-native platform that replaced all three legacy systems with a single, integrated application. The architecture was built for HIPAA compliance from day one, with role-based access control, full audit logging, and encrypted data at rest and in transit. A real-time scheduling engine eliminated double-bookings and gave front desk staff live visibility across all locations simultaneously.

Enterprise 2024 Business Automation

AutoFlow — Enterprise Process Automation System

A mid-size distribution company was processing procurement requests, purchase orders, and supplier invoices entirely through email, spreadsheets, and manual approval chains. Each invoice required an average of 4.5 manual touchpoints across 3 departments before payment was authorized. The finance team was spending over 60% of their working hours on data entry and chasing approvals.

We built a fully automated procurement and invoice workflow engine that integrated with their existing ERP, email system, and document management platform. Rules-based routing handles 80% of standard cases automatically. Complex or high-value transactions are flagged and routed to the right approver with full context already pre-populated — no data entry required.

Infrastructure 2023 Cloud Migration

CloudLift — Zero-Downtime Infrastructure Migration

A SaaS company had grown their product on aging on-premise bare-metal servers over 7 years. Hardware failures were becoming increasingly frequent, deployments required a dedicated maintenance window and manual SSH work, and the team had no visibility into system health beyond basic server uptime monitoring.

We designed a migration path that containerized the monolith without requiring a rewrite, established the cloud environment in parallel, migrated data with zero data loss, and cut over traffic with a blue-green deployment strategy that resulted in zero minutes of production downtime. Post-migration, the team deploys multiple times per day with full observability, automated rollback capability, and infrastructure costs down 40% from bare-metal hosting fees.

AI & Automation 2024 AI System

NexusAI — B2B Support Intelligence System

A B2B SaaS platform with 8,000 active users was receiving 400+ support tickets per week. Their 3-person support team was overwhelmed, first-response times were slipping to 18+ hours, and the team was spending most of their time answering questions already documented in their knowledge base — just not surfaced effectively.

We built a production-grade AI support agent deeply integrated with their existing help desk, knowledge base, and product documentation. The agent handles tier-1 queries autonomously, generates contextual answers from source documentation, tracks conversation state, and routes complex or sensitive cases to human agents with full conversation context pre-loaded. A confidence threshold system ensures uncertain responses always escalate rather than guess.
