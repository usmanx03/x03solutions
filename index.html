Healthcare & Medical

Your patients trust you with their most sensitive information. The software supporting that trust needs to be built accordingly.

We build clinical management systems, patient scheduling platforms, health record integrations, and administrative workflows designed to HIPAA compliance requirements from the ground up — not retrofitted. Every data access is logged. Every record is encrypted. Role-based access ensures the right people see only what they need to.

Healthcare operations don't have a maintenance window. The systems we deliver are built for the uptime and reliability that clinical environments demand.

Payment Systems

Payment processing is one of the highest-risk surfaces in any software system. It demands engineering that treats security as a first-class requirement.

We design and build payment integrations, billing platforms, and financial systems that meet PCI DSS compliance requirements — with proper tokenization, encrypted transmission, secure data handling, and the audit trails that financial operations and regulators require.

Transactions that fail or data that leaks have direct business and legal consequences. Our payment systems are built with that understanding from the first architecture decision.

What we've built in payments

Payment gateway integrations and processing systems
Subscription billing and recurring invoice platforms
Financial reconciliation and reporting systems
Multi-currency and multi-gateway order processing
Secure transaction audit logging
Compliance standard
PCI DSS — Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard
Cardholder data protection, network security, access control, and monitoring built to PCI DSS requirements.
Warehouse & Logistics

Warehouse operations break down when inventory data is inaccurate, delayed, or siloed. The system needs to reflect reality — in real time, across every location.

We build warehouse management systems with real-time inventory tracking, order fulfilment workflows, multi-location visibility, and supplier management integrations that give your operations team the data they need to make decisions without manual reconciliation.

We've built systems that handle high-volume picking and packing operations, automated reorder triggers, and cross-warehouse stock transfers — all connected to the ERP or order management system your business already uses.

What we've built in warehouse & logistics

Warehouse management systems (WMS)
Real-time inventory tracking and control
Multi-location stock visibility and transfers
Order fulfilment and picking workflows
Supplier and purchase order management
ERP and logistics platform integrations
Retail & Apparel

Retail and apparel businesses operate with specific workflows that generic software can't accommodate. The fitting room, the tailor's order book, the customer measurement record — these need custom systems.

We've built tailor management systems, garment order platforms, customer measurement tracking, and production workflow systems designed around the specific operational reality of apparel and retail businesses — not a generic template retrofitted to the industry.

Custom fitting, alterations tracking, fabric inventory, delivery scheduling — the software manages the complexity so your team can focus on the work.

What we've built in retail & apparel

Tailor management and order tracking systems
Customer measurement databases and history
Garment production workflow platforms
Retail order management and delivery tracking
Fabric and material inventory systems
Customer-facing portals and order status tracking
